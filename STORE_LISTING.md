## Description

Know exactly how many days you have left in the Schengen Area — without
handing your travel history to anyone.

Schengen Guard tracks the 90/180-day rule entirely on your device. No
sign-up. No cloud account. No server storing where you've been. Just open
the app and see your number.

**WHY SCHENGEN GUARD**
- No account required — open it and start tracking in seconds
- Your data never leaves your phone — nothing is uploaded, ever
- Works fully offline — check your balance at the border with no wifi needed
- Export your trips any time — your data, your file, your control

**HOW IT WORKS**
Add your past and planned stays. Schengen Guard applies the official
rolling 180-day calculation to show your true balance — how many days
you've used, how many you have left, and when your allowance refreshes.

**WHO IT'S FOR**
Built for UK travellers, digital nomads, second-home owners, and anyone
visiting the Schengen Area visa-free who wants a straight answer without
giving up their privacy to get it.

Schengen Guard is an independent tool and not affiliated with the EU or
any government. Always confirm your position against official records
before travelling.
